About Us

What is Waterwise?

Waterwise is an application designed to help users track, monitor, and reduce their water consumption. By providing insights into everyday water usage, we aim to encourage more sustainable habits and raise awareness about the importance of conserving water.

Our goal is to combine technology with environmental awareness by developing an accessible application that helps people make informed decisions about their water consumption.

Why is Saving Water Important?

Excessive water consumption can have serious consequences for both the environment and society. Overuse of freshwater resources can contribute to water shortages and damage to ecosystems that depend on rivers, lakes, and wetlands. As climate change brings more frequent and severe droughts to many regions, responsible water management is becoming increasingly important. Even in countries where freshwater is generally abundant, such as Sweden, local water shortages can occur, particularly during dry summer months.

Our Project

Waterwise was developed as part of the course DIT257 Agile Software Project Management, taught at the University of Gothenburg and Chalmers University of Technology.

We are a team of five students working together to design and develop the application while gaining practical experience in agile software development, teamwork, and project management.
